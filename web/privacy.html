Draftwall

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Draftwall lets you store and view visual boards, files, and notes. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the service. Contact the operator at lubkin.andrey@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process the content you upload or create, including boards, files, notes, and layout settings. If you use Google sign-in when available, we process your email address and Google account identifier to authenticate you. We request only the openid and email permissions, not access to Gmail, Google Drive, or contacts.

We use a necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. Our hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses, request details, and error logs, to deliver and protect the service.

How information is used and shared

We use information to provide the service, save and display your content, authenticate access, respond to requests, and prevent abuse. Vercel provides hosting and storage; Google provides Google sign-in. These providers process information needed to perform their services. We do not sell personal information or use Google account data for advertising.

People with access to your workspace can view its content. Anyone with a direct file access link may be able to view that file; keep these links private. Uploaded content may include external resources governed by their providers' privacy practices.

Retention and your choices

Content is kept until it is deleted through the service or following a request to us. Technical logs and cached copies may remain for the periods used by our infrastructure providers. We retain information only as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, and address security issues.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email lubkin.andrey@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request. You can also revoke Google access in your Google Account settings; doing so does not automatically delete content stored in Draftwall.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
